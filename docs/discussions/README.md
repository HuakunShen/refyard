# Discussions

Raw records of design conversations, kept because the reasoning matters later even when the
conclusion does not.

A file here is **not a rule**. Nothing in this directory is binding on implementation, and a
later discussion may contradict an earlier one — that is the point of keeping them in order.
When a discussion does settle something, the decision is moved into
`docs/product/north-star.md` (product shape), `AGENTS.md` (how work is done), or the active
plan, and this directory keeps the record of how it was reached.

Why keep them at all:

- a decision without its reasoning gets re-litigated, usually by someone who was not there;
- the alternative that was rejected is often the fastest way to explain why the chosen one is
  shaped the way it is;
- a discussion that ended in "this is probably right, but we cannot verify it here" is exactly
  the kind of thing a later task needs to know before claiming a platform works.
