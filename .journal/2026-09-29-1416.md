# Timestamp: 2026-09-29 14:16 SGT

## Core decision/topic

Correct the Windows ACL test's seeded journal state after CI showed that the
record-byte assertion contradicted Refyard's crash-recovery contract.

## Options considered

- Weaken or remove the production recovery rewrite. Rejected: an `Accepted`
  record is unfinished, and after a process restart the service cannot know
  whether Git ran; converting it to `Unknown` and blocking writes is the
  fail-closed contract.
- Keep an `Accepted` seed and relax the byte comparison. Rejected: the test is
  meant to prove ACL repair does not mutate journal content, but the comparison
  would then conflate that with the separate, required recovery transition.
- Seed an already terminal operation through `Journal::mark_cancelled`, then
  compare bytes across embedded-host open. Chosen: the journal remains a valid
  private record, startup has no recovery transition to perform, and the test
  isolates the ACL/privacy behavior it is meant to verify.

## Final decision & rationale

The Windows failure is a test-fixture error, not an ACL production regression.
Keep the production recovery policy intact and construct a terminal journal
record through the public journal transition before testing byte preservation.
